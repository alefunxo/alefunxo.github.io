01

About

I am a researcher and advisor working on the integration of distributed energy resources, including photovoltaics, battery storage, electric vehicles, and flexibility strategies. My work combines modeling, data analysis, and system-level thinking to address questions related to grid constraints, local energy system design, and the operational value of flexibility.

What I bring

I bridge advanced technical analysis and practical decision-making. My background includes low-voltage networks, vehicle-to-grid integration, storage, local energy systems, and decision-support tools for energy transition planning.

Core topics

  • Renewable integration and local energy systems
  • Battery storage and flexibility strategies
  • Electric vehicles and vehicle-to-grid
  • Low-voltage networks and congestion
  • Data-driven tools for energy decision-making
02

What decisions I help inform

I support organizations facing concrete energy transition decisions, including:

  • Should we invest in PV, storage, or both?
  • How will electrification, including EVs and heat pumps, affect local constraints and costs?
  • What flexibility options are available, and what is their operational or economic value?
  • How can energy data support better planning, operation, or investment decisions?

My work combines system-level modeling, scenario evaluation, and quantitative analysis to provide clear evidence for these questions.
