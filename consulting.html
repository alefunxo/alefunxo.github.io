Consulting

Alongside my research, I take on focused advisory engagements — bringing the same modeling and data-analysis approach to concrete decisions organizations are facing on renewables, storage, electrification, and grid constraints.

For enterprises

  • Assessment of renewable integration strategies
  • Evaluation of storage and flexibility opportunities
  • EV charging and fleet electrification analysis
  • Energy data analysis and scenario comparison

For public and research actors

  • Technical support in project design and evaluation
  • Development of decision-support methodologies
  • Urban and district energy system analysis
  • Translation of technical results for stakeholders

How engagements typically work

Most engagements start with a clearly framed question — should we invest in PV, storage, or both; how will electrification affect local constraints; what is flexibility worth under our tariffs and operating patterns. I combine system-level modeling, scenario evaluation, and quantitative analysis to provide clear, defensible evidence for that question, and communicate results in a form that supports the decision at hand rather than a general technical report.
