Research projects

DUT — awarded, January 2026 - January 2029

Interdisciplinary methodology to evaluate and implement Positive Energy Districts in Europe, integrating energy system modeling, urban planning, and social acceptance. Helped coordinate, developed the project concept, and co-wrote the pre-proposal and proposal. Total requested funding: 1.4M EUR (350,000 CHF for HES-SO Valais/Wallis).

SFOE Grids — Dig-A-Plan — awarded, finished

Risk-based tool for the reinforcement, replacement, and expansion of distribution grid assets under uncertain conditions. Wrote and discussed the EPFL involvement across both proposal phases. Total requested funding: 286,670 CHF (75,235 CHF for EPFL).

Inter-institution Valais — OpenBEERS — awarded, finished

Facilitated the energy transition in the Canton Valais by standardizing building data storage, supporting DER integration, and enhancing energy performance simulations. Coordinated, developed the project concept, and wrote the full proposal. Total requested funding: 150,000 CHF (75,000 CHF for HES-SO Valais/Wallis).
